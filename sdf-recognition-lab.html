Analysis by synthesis. One object is rendered at a hidden orientation drawn uniformly from SO(3), then blurred and occluded. That degraded image is the observation. To recognize it, the lab re-renders every shape across a grid that covers the whole rotation group and keeps the closest match, then refines the winner locally. In-plane roll costs no renders at all, because rolling the camera about its own axis only spins the image. There is no training here, only a fast renderer and a search.

THE SEARCH SPACE

Colours are display only. Every buffer the search actually compares is rendered in one neutral grey, the same for all five shapes, so a match can never be a colour match.
